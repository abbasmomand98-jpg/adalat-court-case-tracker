CASE BOARD

Know your case. Never miss a hearing.

Pakistan's courts carry about 2.3 million pending cases. Ahmed Raza, 42, Rawalpindi, has an inherited-property case pending 3 years — he missed one hearing when the date changed without notice and paid Rs 15,000 per hearing with no receipts. Adalat tracks every case by its number: the next hearing, the stage, and a countdown — so no date ever moves without you knowing.

COST LEDGER

A receipt for every rupee.

Civil Suit 1284/2024 has cost Rs 87,500 across 9 hearings against a Rs 75,000 estimate — Rs 12,500 over. Lawyer fees are Rs 72,000 (9 hearings × Rs 8,000). The official court fee is Rs 500. But Rs 10,000 of "misc facilitation" has no receipt — Adalat flags it, so you can ask for the receipt before you pay it again.

FEE SPOTTER

Spot the fee traps.

Three things a lawyer does that should stop you hiring him — and one safe habit. A full fee upfront in cash with no written agreement; a "guaranteed win" promise; and a Rs 5,000 "court fee" when the official fee is Rs 500. Call each one a red flag or safe, then check your score.
